what helps

What helps

app version
Android settings, Apps, Netglow. The version is near the bottom of that page. It also shows under About in Netglow Settings.
phone model
Android settings, About phone.
Android version
Android settings, About phone, Android version.
screen
Network, Cameras, Apps, a tool, Settings, the names file, Timeline, Shizuku mode, Pro or the widget.
steps
What you did, one step per line, so we can do the same.
expected and actual
What you thought would happen, and what happened.
a picture
A screenshot or a short screen recording. Drag it into the form.

Netglow shows the names and addresses of devices on your Wi-Fi. Before you attach a screenshot, crop or cover anything private, like device names, addresses and hardware addresses. Issues on GitHub are public.
